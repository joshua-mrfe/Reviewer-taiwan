Lesson 1

What is MDAS?

MDAS tells us the order to follow when solving a math expression.

M
Multiplication ×
D
Division ÷
A
Addition +
S
Subtraction −
Remember: Multiplication and division are solved from left to right. Addition and subtraction are also solved from left to right.
Lesson 2

What is PEMDAS?

PEMDAS adds parentheses and exponents to the order of operations.

P
Parentheses ( )
E
Exponents ²
M/D
Multiply & Divide
A/S
Add & Subtract

Correct order: Parentheses → Exponents → Multiplication/Division → Addition/Subtraction.

Important: MD and AS are equal partners. Do not always multiply before dividing or add before subtracting.

Worked Examples

Example 1: MDAS

10 + 20 ÷ 5 × 2
Step 1: Divide → 20 ÷ 5 = 4
Step 2: Multiply → 4 × 2 = 8
Step 3: Add → 10 + 8 = 18

Example 2: PEMDAS with parentheses

(6 + 2) × 3
Step 1: Parentheses → 6 + 2 = 8
Step 2: Multiply → 8 × 3 = 24

Example 3: Exponents

2 + 3² × 2
Step 1: Exponent → 3² = 9
Step 2: Multiply → 9 × 2 = 18
Step 3: Add → 2 + 18 = 20

Example 4: Left to right

24 ÷ 6 × 2
Step 1: Divide → 24 ÷ 6 = 4
Step 2: Multiply → 4 × 2 = 8

Do not multiply first just because M appears before D in PEMDAS.
